01 — Who We Are

About Us

At ImmunoHuman Research, every trial we conduct is anchored in a profound respect for human life. Beyond protocols and data, our true purpose is transforming patient care by helping discover safer, more effective treatments for chronic and life‑threatening illnesses. By maintaining rigorous safety standards and putting trial participants first, we help turn scientific potential into real‑world cures — giving patients more time, better quality of life, and a healthier future for generations to come.

We work at the intersection of immunology and human‑centred care. Our name carries that intent: Immuno, for the biology we study and harness, and Human, for the person at the centre of every protocol we write. Every study we run is built on informed consent that is genuinely understood, monitoring that never cuts corners, and reporting that stakeholders and regulators can rely on without question.

03 — How A Trial Moves

From Molecule to Medicine

Clinical development is a strict sequence — each phase must be satisfied before the next begins.

Phase I

Safety

A small group of volunteers receives the investigational treatment to assess safety, dosage range, and how the body processes it.

Phase II

Efficacy

A larger patient group tests whether the treatment works as intended, while safety monitoring continues in parallel.

Phase III

Confirmation

Large, often multi‑centre trials confirm effectiveness, monitor side effects, and compare against existing standards of care.

Phase IV

Surveillance

After approval, long‑term monitoring tracks real‑world safety and outcomes across a wider population.

Mr. Bhorkar comes from a Clinical Research Industry background, and leads ImmunoHuman Research with a clear conviction: trials should be run ethically, backed by proper data management, at every stage.
